Alfred OpenClaw Assistant

Alfred is a privately operated AI assistant built using the OpenClaw platform.

The application is intended for personal use by its owner. It helps organize information, manage personal productivity, and interact with connected services.

Google Workspace Integration

Alfred connects to Google Workspace services using Google OAuth 2.0 authorization.

Supported services include:

  • Gmail — searching, reading and organizing email.
  • Google Calendar — viewing and managing calendar events.
  • Google Drive — locating and accessing files.
  • Google Docs — accessing and editing documents.
  • Google Sheets — accessing and managing spreadsheets.

Access is limited to the permissions explicitly granted by the account owner. Operations that modify data or send communications require the owner's approval.

Privacy and Security

Alfred is not a public service and does not offer accounts or access to third-party users. Google Workspace data may be processed by external AI providers when necessary to fulfill the owner's requests.

For additional information, see the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
