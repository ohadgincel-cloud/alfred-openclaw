Privacy Policy

Alfred OpenClaw Assistant

Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Overview

Alfred OpenClaw Assistant is a privately operated AI assistant intended for the personal use of its owner. It is not offered as a public service.

2. Google Account Access

With the account owner's authorization, Alfred may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets through Google OAuth 2.0.

3. Use of Google Data

Google Workspace information is used to respond to the owner's requests, including retrieving emails, checking calendar events, locating files and working with documents.

Google data is not sold or used for advertising. It is not used to train general-purpose AI models by the application operator.

4. AI Processing

To fulfill certain requests, relevant information may be transmitted to external AI model providers for processing. Only information needed for the requested task should be shared. Processing by these providers is subject to their applicable privacy and data handling policies.

5. Storage and Security

Alfred operates in a privately managed computing environment. Authentication credentials and operational data are stored in that environment and are not published through this website.

Reasonable security measures are used to protect credentials and connected account information. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Operational data may be retained as needed for the assistant's functionality and maintenance. The owner controls the local environment and can remove stored data and revoke Google access.

7. Third-Party Access

Alfred is not available for registration by third parties. Google data is not shared for advertising or resale. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary for the owner's requested functionality.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the application's functionality or data handling.
